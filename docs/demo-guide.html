PAGE 3: Core Operations Modules & Inventory Ledger

High-Velocity Ledgering: In discount retail, physical inventory must balance down to the milligram, checkouts must never hang on locks, and stock in transit must be fully accounted for. RetailHub implements high-precision, optimistic concurrency control and immutable double-entry movement ledgering.

1. Catalog Management & Barcode-First Ingestion

Barcode-First Register Search

Registers scan standard 13-digit EAN/UPC barcodes. The search engine resolves exact barcode matches in < 10 ms, falling back to tri-gram partial text across SKU and description only when unbarcoded items are queried.

D-Mart Value-Pricing Validation

Strict catalog validation formula: MRP ≥ Selling Price ≥ Cost Price. Prevents negative gross margins, loss-making onboarding, and legal non-compliance across all 500 catalog items.

2. Double-Entry Inventory Movement Ledger

Physical inventory balances are never directly updated without a corresponding row in the InventoryMovement table. This provides a forensic, tamper-proof audit trail for every gram of product:

INWARD_RECEIPT (+)
Direct intake from Central Distribution Center or supplier PO.
POS_SALE (-)
Point-of-sale register checkouts deduct shelf inventory.
TRANSFER_OUT_DISPATCH (-)
Stock loaded on carrier; moves from available to in-transit.
TRANSFER_IN_RECEIPT (+)
Dock inward scan confirms shipment receipt at destination.
DAMAGED_WRITE_OFF (-)
Auto-written off upon road transit shortfall or dock damage.
STOCK_ADJUSTMENT (±)
Physical cycle count variance with mandatory reason codes.

3. The 3-Step Stock Transfer State Machine

Eliminates stock leakage during inter-store truck transit through three strictly audited states:

PENDING_APPROVAL
Proposal created by AI or Store Manager.
Stock unchanged at both stores.
→
APPROVED_IN_TRANSIT
Approved by Regional Manager.
Donor stock ↓ | In-Transit stock ↑
Carrier & Vehicle Number assigned.
→
RECEIVED
Dock barcode scan verifies shipment.
In-Transit stock ↓ | Dest stock ↑
Shortfalls → Auto DAMAGED_WRITE_OFF.

4. Rolling Sales Velocity (ADS) Calculation

Rolling ADS (Units/Day) = (Total Quantity Sold over past 30 days) / 30.0

Captured continuously from POS checkout transactions. If an item has 0 sales in 30 days, ADS is strictly 0.000, producing a dormant DOI of 999.0 days (preventing false alarms). If positive sales exist, DOI immediately reflects live customer velocity.

PAGE 4: Intelligence Engine & Recommendation Generation

Deterministic Runout & Proximity: RetailHub generates recommendations using mathematical formulas rather than black-box guesses. Runout risk is determined by physical velocity; donor stores are ranked by Haversine road proximity; and transfers are strictly clamped by the 7-day donor safety floor.

1. Days of Inventory (DOI) & Stock Health Tiers

DOI = Available Stock / Rolling 30-Day ADS
CRITICAL ZONE
< 3.0 Days
Immediate stockout risk; revenue bleed occurring; triggers High Priority rebalance.
LOW ZONE
3.0 to 7.0 Days
Approaching reorder point; replenishment candidate to avoid stockout.
HEALTHY ZONE
7.0 to 30.0 Days
Optimal operational equilibrium; maximum working capital efficiency.
EXCESS ZONE
> 30.0 Days
Surplus dormant stock tying up capital; designated primary candidate donor.

2. Autonomous Generation: The Background Engine Safeguards

The 7-Day Donor Safety Floor Clamp

Solving a stockout at Store A must never cause a secondary stockout at Store B. The rebalance algorithm verifies:

Post-Transfer DOI (Donor) = (Donor Stock - Transfer Qty) / Donor ADS ≥ 7.0 Days

If transferring the requested quantity causes the donor store's buffer to drop below 7.0 days, the transferable stock is automatically clamped to only what is genuinely surplus.

Haversine Road Proximity Ranking

Candidate donors must reside in the same regional cluster. Proximity is ranked using Great-Circle road distance:

d = 2R · arcsin(√(sin²(Δlat/2) + cos(lat1)cos(lat2)sin²(Δlon/2)))

Nearest eligible donor is prioritized to minimize truck transit time and logistics costs. If no peer qualifies, the system defaults to Central Supply Fallback.

3. Manual Generation: Store Manager Request Workflow

Floor associates identify surge demand early:

  • Initiation: Store Manager clicks "New Store Request" in Action Center.
  • Inputs: Selects Product SKU, enters required quantity, sets priority (`HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, `LOW`), and enters operational rationale (e.g., "Weekend promotional display").
  • System Action: Persists record with isManualRequest = true and tags requesting user ID. Appears immediately in the Regional Manager's queue under the "Store Requests" tab.

PAGE 5: Action Center & Human-in-the-Loop Execution

The Operational Cockpit: The Action Center provides complete contextual transparency before a manager commits physical inventory. It features side-by-side DOI comparisons, interactive candidate store selectors, and four audited action pathways.

1. Action Center Interface Anatomy

1. Side-by-Side Comparison

Displays Destination vs. Donor store metrics side-by-side: Current stock vs. Post-Transfer stock, Current DOI vs. Projected DOI, and visual color gauges.

2. AI Narrative Card

Explains why the action is optimal, citing exact days of inventory, safety stock buffers, road distance (km), and projected transit time.

3. Candidate Store Selector

Allows the manager to override the AI's donor choice, listing all regional peer stores ranked by proximity with live safety floor eligibility.

2. The 4 Operational Decision Pathways

Decision Pathway Operational Trigger & Workflow System Execution & Database Impact
1. Single Approval
(APPROVE)		 Manager reviews proposal drawer and clicks "Approve Transfer". Creates StockTransfer in APPROVED_IN_TRANSIT status. Decrements donor available stock; increments in-transit stock; logs TRANSFER_OUT_DISPATCH. Recommendation marked APPROVED.
2. Batch Approval
(BATCH_APPROVE)		 Manager selects 5–50 recommendations via table checkboxes and clicks "Approve Selected (N)" on the floating bar. Processes all selected items in an atomic database transaction. Generates consolidated transfer manifests and updates total approved valuation.
3. Modification
(MODIFY)		 Manager adjusts transfer quantity or selects an alternative donor store. Mandatory operational rationale required. Re-validates the 7-day donor safety floor. If proposed quantity breaches the donor's 7-day buffer, system rejects modification. Updates status to MODIFIED.
4. Rejection
(REJECT)		 Manager clicks "Reject". System requires a mandatory business justification before committing. Transitions status to REJECTED. Records full rationale in ActionHistory and audit log. Prevents redundant generation for this cycle.

3. Automated Transfer Execution & Inward Dock Receiving

Carrier Dispatch & In-Transit Tracking

Upon approval, transfers receive a tracking code (e.g. TRF-C1-849201), vehicle registration number, and carrier assignment (e.g. BlueDart Express Retail Logistics). Physical inventory is quarantined in-transit so neither store accidentally sells it.

Dock Scans & Shortfall Write-Off

Destination store scans incoming shipment. If full quantity is received, destination available stock increments. If transit damage occurs (e.g. 20 dispatched, 18 received), system accepts 18 units and automatically logs an audited DAMAGED_WRITE_OFF for 2 units.

4. Autonomous Self-Healing & Auto-Expiration

If a store manager leaves a recommendation open, but the store subsequently receives a separate direct shipment from Central Supply that raises inventory above the reorder point:

If Current Stock > Reorder Level → Recommendation Status transitions to AUTO_EXPIRED

The background engine auto-closes the recommendation with reason: "Auto-closed: Shelf stock replenished via inward receipt."
